**Author**:  Jeff Pepper, Xiao Hui Wang 

**Format**: Audiobook (Narrators:  Jeff Pepper, Xiao Hui Wang)


_The Journey to the West in Easy Chinese_ is exactly what it sounds like. It is kinda just Journey to the West with simplified language. The conceit is that every chapter adds some number of (apparently ~50) new words, meaning that the first chapters were insultingly easy for me, and the last chapters were just about my comprehension level as someone who primarily knows Mandarin from my family basically + I learned a good number of (mostly Buddhism or demon related...) words along the way. 

The book itself seems mildly abridged, though it's hard to tell how much is just the "simplification" of the language. It's also basically a bunch of separate "easy Chinese" primers stapled together, so there are possibly unnecessary intros/outtros to each chapter (the narrator spends like the first 40% of the book literally being like hello child its time for your bedtime story, which I'm _pretty_ sure is not in the original). The production itself is not amazing, though it's acceptable enough. In the audiobook at least, there are, e.g., some weird repeated (uncut outtakes, presumably) phrases and, for some reason, whenever there's a new chapter title, an _English_ narrator says "Chapter X: Y" instead of just, having it in Chinese.
